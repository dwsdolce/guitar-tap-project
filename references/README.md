# References

Third-party and background reference material that informed Guitar Tap's DSP, material
model, and UI across all three editions — kept here as shared project reference rather
than in any one edition's repo. Mostly not authored by the project; retained for provenance.

## Pitch detection & spectrum display
- **`2002_JASA_YIN.pdf`** — de Cheveigné & Kawahara, *YIN, a fundamental frequency estimator for speech and music* (JASA, 2002).
- **`Comparing Pitch Detection Algorithms vs Neural Network.pdf`** — survey comparing conventional pitch-detection algorithms with a neural-network approach.
- **`Fast and Efficient Pitch Detection - Cycfi Research.webloc`** — bookmark to the Cycfi Research article.
- **`Spectra.png`** — a downloaded FFT-spectra comparison (two annotated tap-tone captures); a spectrum-display / annotation-style reference.

## Material properties & tonewood
- **`tonewood-references.md`** — curated index of the acoustic-property values, quality thresholds, and equations behind `MaterialProperties.swift` / `material_properties.py` (Blevins free-free beam formula, Gore, etc.).
- **`Trevor Gore Thickness.docx`** + **`Trevor Gore's guitar plate thicknessing equations - Claude.pdf`** — Gore's plate-thicknessing method and equations.
- **`Brace Young's Modulus Derivation.docx`** — derivation behind the brace Young's-modulus calculation.
- **`Modes.docx`** — vibration-mode notes.
- **`Wood Property Measurementsl.xlsx`** — tonewood property measurements.
- **`Key Metrics from Tap Testing.docx`** — metrics gathered from tap testing.

Assembled during the post-standup cleanup (2026-08-03), from `guitar_tap/docs/` and `GuitarTap/`.
